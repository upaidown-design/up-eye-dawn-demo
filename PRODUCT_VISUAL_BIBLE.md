# Product visual bible — draft / approval required

## Rover (`ROVER_001`)

Compact tracked industrial platform; dark graphite body with restrained white technical panels; two front cameras plus a central sensor zone; one rear/top communications mast; low rectangular articulated drone bay; front deployable multipin probe; non-anthropomorphic form; no character face/eyes; small configurable logo decal; amber/green status lights. Track and body proportions must be locked from an approved reference sheet before batch generation.

## Sentinel (`SENTINEL_003`)

Grounded industrial base, slender mast and lightweight weatherproof upper module with protected landing surface and hinged/sliding cover. Neutral aluminium/graphite/white palette, minimal status lighting, separate meteo and communications antennas. Stage count, max height, door motion and sensor count remain configuration-driven and `NEEDS_VERIFICATION`.

Concept visuals never prove dimensions, hardware existence or performance. Interface labels and branding are overlays, not baked into renders.
