# Visual asset plan

Gate: no production render is approved until `PRODUCT_VISUAL_BIBLE.md` is approved and reference ownership is reviewed.

- Rover product: eight canonical views (front/rear 3/4, front/rear/left/right/top/isometric) plus exploded view.
- Rover states: bay closed/open, drone stored/takeoff/landing, probe retracted/deployed/in soil, driving/stopped/charging.
- Rover environments: vineyard, olive grove, almond/pistachio, broadacre, farm road, wet/dry soil, golden hour and neutral technical daylight.
- Sentinel: stowed/raising/raised, bay closed/open, drone stored/takeoff/return, maintenance, front/side/rear/top/3/4, exploded/concept drawing and farm integration.
- Deliverables: clean renders without embedded labels; hero, transparent PNG/WebP, thumbnail, mobile and 4K variants where supported.
- Metadata required: asset ID, date, generator, prompt, references, classification, status and approval.

Missing priorities: approved rover identity views, exact mast stage count, licensed drone geometry, probe mechanics, AgileX licensing/CAD decision, and proof photographs clearly separated from concept renders.
