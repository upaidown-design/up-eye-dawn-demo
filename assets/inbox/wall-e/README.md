# Rover asset inbox

Accepted: `.glb` preferred; `.gltf` with local textures; approved `.png/.webp` references. Recommended GLB under 15 MB, under 150k triangles, 2K textures, origin centered at ground contact, +Y up, +Z forward. Expected filename: `rover-approved-v1.glb`.

Provide source, author, owner, license/authorization and checksum. Mark each image `CONCEPT_RENDER` or `PHYSICAL_PROTOTYPE`; prototype claims require date, device identity and owner approval. The application will register the asset in `assets/manifest/assets-manifest.json` and retain the procedural fallback.
