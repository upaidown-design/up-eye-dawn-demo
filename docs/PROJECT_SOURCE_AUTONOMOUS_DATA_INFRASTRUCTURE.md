## Narrative captured from the source

The presentation frames UPAIDOWN as a closed-loop agricultural intelligence infrastructure spanning four coordinated layers:

- air for programmed macro observation and early detection;
- land for close terrestrial inspection;
- soil for georeferenced physical measurements;
- cloud for historical memory, correlation, AI analysis and decision support.

Its core operating narrative is that aerial detection identifies a location, the terrestrial rover navigates to it, a physical inspection supplies ground truth and the combined observations become a longitudinal data asset.
